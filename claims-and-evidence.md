# Claims and Evidence Register

Use this file to keep public descriptions and the UNICEF application accurate.

| Topic | Safe wording at concept stage | Evidence needed for a stronger claim |
|---|---|---|
| Technology | “We propose to assess biomass pyrolysis.” | Reviewed design and documented validation. |
| Production | “The intended product streams are bio-oil, gas, and char.” | Traceable test records showing actual outputs. |
| Diesel replacement | “Bio-oil upgrading may be explored with qualified partners.” | Upgrading process, applicable standards, and independent fuel testing. |
| Environmental benefit | “The project aims to investigate potential waste and environmental benefits.” | Feedstock sustainability assessment, emissions data, and life-cycle analysis. |
| Community impact | “The project intends to create inclusive livelihood opportunities.” | Documented participation, outcomes, and impact evaluation. |
| Commercial viability | “Commercial feasibility remains to be assessed.” | Transparent cost model, market evidence, and validated performance. |
| Current progress | “The project is at concept and planning stage.” | Update only when work is completed and documented. |

## Update practice
For every future milestone, record the date, what was done, who carried it out, the method, results, limitations, and supporting documents. Distinguish estimates from measurements and third-party findings from the project team's own observations.
