Pearls EcoFuel Solutions

 Proposed biomass pyrolysis and circular-economy initiative

Organization: Pearls for Change Foundation
Project lead: Stacy Grace Akinyi
Proposed location: Kisumu County, Kenya
Repository purpose: Project documentation and future technical records

 Overview

Pearls EcoFuel Solutions is a proposed community-focused initiative to assess whether suitable agricultural residues and other responsibly sourced biomass can be converted through pyrolysis into useful product streams: bio-oil, non-condensable gas, and solid char.

The project aims to investigate local waste-to-value opportunities while prioritizing environmental protection, technical feasibility, worker and community safety, and livelihood opportunities for women and young people.

 Proposed feedstocks for assessment

Potential materials to assess include rice husks, maize cobs, sugarcane bagasse, water hyacinth, coffee husks, and other locally available residues. No material is assumed suitable until its availability, moisture, ash and contaminant profile, competing uses, collection impacts, and processing requirements have been assessed.

 Conceptual product streams

Bio-oil: a complex liquid product that requires characterization and safe handling. It is not automatically equivalent to diesel or ready for use in engines.
Syn gas-Non-condensable gas: a potentially combustible process stream that requires engineered containment, monitoring, and safe management.
Eco Briquettes- Char: a carbon-rich solid whose quality and appropriate end use must be tested before use or sale.

Product proportions and properties vary with feedstock and technology. No yield claims are made in this repository.

Proposed development phases

1. Feasibility: map feedstock availability, competing uses, community priorities, and possible partners.
2. Technical assessment: compare suitable process options with qualified engineers and research partners.
3. Safeguards and approvals: identify environmental, occupational safety, fire, air-quality, waste-management, and other applicable requirements.
4. Controlled validation: if approved and adequately resourced, conduct testing with qualified technical partners and document measured results.
5. Business and impact assessment: evaluate costs, product quality, market demand, and social and environmental outcomes before considering scale-up.

 Safeguards

The project will not claim that products are clean, safe, carbon-neutral, or commercially viable without evidence. Any future trials should be planned and supervised by qualified professionals in suitable facilities, with appropriate approvals, risk assessments, monitoring, and emergency arrangements. Do not build or operate a pyrolysis system based on this concept document alone.

 Proposed monitoring indicators

- Feedstock types assessed and documented.
- Evidence of sustainable supply and competing-use analysis.
- Technical and implementation partners engaged.
- Approvals and safeguards identified or obtained, where applicable.
- Product characterization and emissions results, if testing occurs.
- Cost estimates and market assessment.
- Community consultation and participation, including women and youth.
