# Proposed 12-Month Roadmap

**Status:** Planning document; schedule subject to funding, partnerships, approvals, and feasibility findings.

| Period | Proposed activities | Evidence / deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–3 | Consult communities; map candidate feedstocks and current uses; identify technical partners; review local requirements. | Consultation notes; feedstock screening matrix; partner list; preliminary risk register. |
| Months 4–6 | Compare technology pathways with qualified specialists; assess logistics, process requirements, and potential product uses. | Options assessment; preliminary process concept; initial cost categories; list of required tests and approvals. |
| Months 7–9 | If feasible, funded, approved, and supervised by qualified partners, undertake controlled validation in an appropriate facility. | Test plan; documented approvals; traceable measurements; product characterization and emissions results, if testing proceeds. |
| Months 10–12 | Review technical, environmental, social, and financial evidence; consult stakeholders; decide whether to continue, redesign, or stop. | Feasibility report; updated risk register; market and cost assessment; next-stage decision. |

## Proposed indicators
- Number of feedstocks screened using a documented method.
- Number of community consultations and participants, recorded with appropriate consent.
- Number of qualified technical or research partners engaged.
- Completion of a safeguards and approvals review.
- Availability of traceable test data, only if testing occurs.
- Completion of a preliminary techno-economic assessment.
- A documented go/no-go decision based on evidence.

## Dependencies and decision gates
Progression to testing depends on technical feasibility, adequate funding, qualified supervision, suitable facilities, risk controls, and required approvals. If these conditions are not met, the project should remain at the research and planning stage.
